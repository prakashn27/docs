## How to Add the Widget Script

1. In the Weebly editor, go to **Settings > SEO**.
2. Scroll down to the **Footer Code** section.
3. Copy your widget script from your agent's **Channels** page at [supportunicorn.com](https://supportunicorn.com) and paste it here.

4. Click **Save** and **Publish** your site.

<Note>
  If you need the script on specific pages only, use the **Embed Code** element. Drag it onto the page in the editor and paste the script inside.
</Note>
