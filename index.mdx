## What is Support Unicorn?

Support Unicorn is an AI agent for customer support that helps you provide exceptional customer service across multiple channels. Deploy AI agents that answer customer questions instantly, escalate to human agents when needed, and learn from your knowledge base.

## How It Works

### 1. Create Your AI Agent

Set up your AI agent in minutes. Configure its personality, tone, and behavior to match your brand.

### 2. Build Your Knowledge Base

Upload documents, create Q&A pairs, or scrape your existing documentation. Your AI agent learns from all your content.

### 3. Connect Communication Channels

Add channels where customers can reach you:
- **Live Chat Widget** on your website
- **SMS & WhatsApp** via Twilio
- More channels coming soon

### 4. Set Up Escalations

Configure when and how to escalate conversations to your human support team via Slack, Zendesk, Jira, or Microsoft Teams.

### 5. Go Live

Deploy your AI agent and start providing instant, accurate customer support around the clock.
