## How to Add the Widget Script

1. Click the **Settings** icon (gear) in the top right of your Framer project.
2. Go to the **General** section.
3. Scroll down to **Custom Code**.
4. Copy your widget script from your agent's **Channels** page at [supportunicorn.com](https://supportunicorn.com) and paste it into the **End of `<body>`** block.

5. Click **Save** and **Publish** your site.
